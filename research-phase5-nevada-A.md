## 1. Joël Robuchon — MGM Grand, Las Vegas

**Official restaurant page (verbatim):**
https://mgmgrand.mgmresorts.com/en/restaurants/joel-robuchon-french-restaurant.html
**Hotel page (verbatim):** https://mgmgrand.mgmresorts.com/
**Reservations:** No OpenTable listing found for the main dining room. Book through the official MGM Grand restaurant page (MGM Rewards Gold+ members get priority online reservations) or by phone: **702.891.7925** (large parties 7+: 702.891.1133; general: 877.880.0880).

**Michelin:** ★★ Two Michelin Stars in the inaugural 2026 Michelin Guide Southwest (only two-star restaurant in the Southwest edition; ceremony at Fontainebleau, Aug 2026). General Manager Sean Christopher also received the 2026 Michelin Service Award. Guide.michelin.com listing URL could not be confirmed via search results (Michelin site is JS-rendered; no verbatim URL was returned by any tool). The 2026 two-star award is verified from MGM Resorts' official press release (newsroom.mgmresorts.com, Aug 2026) and the restaurant's own page.

**Address:** 3799 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109 (inside MGM Grand)
**Phone:** 702.891.7925
**Executive Chef:** Eleazar Villanueva (stated on MGM's official page; named a 2025 James Beard Award finalist, Best Chef: Southwest)
**Hours:** Tue–Sat, 5:00 PM – 9:30 PM; closed Monday and Sunday (per official MGM page)
**Cuisine / price:** French, $$$$ (MGM page; Google price level "very expensive")

**Signature dishes & prices (with dates):**
- 16-course Dégustation menu: **$445/person** — reported in a diner review on urcomped.com (undated, likely 2024–2025); another 2026-published site reports the 16-course degustation at "nearly $500/person"
- Tasting menu, dinner for two **$525** (tasting only) with wine pairings **$225–$950** — from a Marché Bacchus blog citing the **April 2025** MGM Grand published menu
- Older tiers (Fine Dining Explorer gallery, ~2019): 16+ course degustation $450, 6-course $240, 3-course $160 — dated era, included for historical reference only
- Signature dishes (names only, official page, no à la carte prices published): Le Caviar Impérial (osetra caviar atop crab in crustacean gelée with cauliflower purée), Le Crab Royal (farm chicken egg stuffed with king crab and osetra caviar), Les Asperges (green asparagus, tomato water, seasonal vegetables, caviar), the legendary pommes purée (silky 2:1 potato-to-butter mashed potatoes), truffled langoustine ravioli

**Notable facts:**
1. Opened 2006 — the first US restaurant of the late Joël Robuchon, who held 32 Michelin stars at his peak (the most ever awarded to one chef). Las Vegas's first 3-star Michelin restaurant in the 2008–2009 Vegas guides; now 2 stars in the 2026 Southwest guide.
2. Intimate 12-table dining room designed to evoke a luxurious Art Deco townhouse with a garden terrace, marble floors, and purple velvet — "Dinner here is an event" (Michelin Guide).
3. Holds Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star (2026 & 2025), AAA Five Diamond, and Wine Spectator Grand Award — one of few US restaurants to hold all three.
4. Famous dessert and bread carts — a rolling three-tiered bread cart with over a dozen breads (Comté cheese bread, Gruyère brioche, saffron focaccia, etc.) bookends the meal with a dessert/mignardise cart.

2. Overlooks the Lake of Dreams with a 90-foot waterfall and nightly multimedia shows; open-air terrace is the prime-view seat (not guaranteed; request on booking).
3. One of only three restaurants in the US offering certified authentic Japanese Kobe beef (alongside one other Wynn Las Vegas restaurant).
4. Chef Mark LoRusso reinterpreted the classic American steakhouse; the restaurant's seafood tower and bone-marrow flambé are guest-favorite showpieces. Children under 5 are not permitted.

**Official restaurant page (verbatim):**
https://www.caesars.com/caesars-palace/restaurants/restaurant-guy-savoy?y_source=1_NDM2Njg3Ny03MTUtbG9jYXRpb24ud2Vic2l0ZQ%3D%3D

**Reservations (OpenTable, verbatim):**

(caesars.com page direct fetch failed in this pass; OpenTable listing confirms bookability)
**Michelin:** ★ One Michelin Star, 2026 Michelin Guide Southwest (confirmed via Caesars' Business Wire press release, Aug 2026). Previously two stars in the 2008–2009 Vegas guides. Guide.michelin.com listing URL could not be confirmed via search results (no verbatim URL returned by tools).

**Address:** 3570 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109 (second floor, Augustus Tower, Caesars Palace)
**Phone:** 702.731.7286
**Executive Chef:** Not publicly stated on current materials found; Chef Guy Savoy himself leads (gusteau.io doc ~2014-era names Mathieu Chartron as chef de cuisine — dated, do not use without re-verification)
**Hours:** Tue–Sat, 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM (per Caesars location data; OpenTable lists Tue–Sat 5:00 pm–8:45 pm)
**Cuisine / price:** French, Contemporary French — OpenTable "$50 and over"

**Signature dishes & prices (with dates):**
- Prestige Tasting Menu (14 courses): **$385/person** — johnchow.com review (2025-era video post; "dinner for two came to over $1,000")
- Chef's tasting menu food-only **~$300/person**, optional wine pairing **$555** — diner review on in.worldorgs.com (undated user review, likely 2024–2025)
- Celebration menu **$555/person**, six-course white truffle menu **$455/person**, optional wine pairing **$250/person** — Las Vegas Magazine, Nov 2020 (dated era)
- Older menus (gusteau.io PDF, ~2014): Innovation-Inspiration 14-course $348; Signature 9-course $258; 3-course Pre-Theatre $120; Krug Chef's Table ~$500/person — historical only
- Special event: "Chef Guy Savoy Presents: Symphony Menu" **$680/person without wine pairing, $1,000/person with premium pairing + cognac** — limited run Nov 18–21, 2026, via OpenTable (chef-hosted, includes photo, signed book, kitchen tour)
- Signature dishes (names only): Artichoke and Black Truffle Soup with toasted mushroom brioche and truffle butter; Colors of Caviar (layered caviar parfait with caviar vinaigrette and caviar sabayon); the legendary rolling bread cart

**Notable facts:**
1. Opened May 2006; celebrated its **20th anniversary in 2026** — Chef Guy Savoy's only restaurant outside France (flagship in Paris at Monnaie de Paris).
2. Awards: 2026 Michelin Guide Southwest One Star; Forbes Five-Star, AAA Five Diamond, and Wine Spectator Grand Award every year since 2008; named highest-ranking Las Vegas restaurant in La Liste's 2026 Top 1,000 (Dec 2025); OpenTable Icons 2024, 2025, 2026.
3. Dining room sits in a quiet corner on the Augustus Tower's second floor with Strip views; the famous bread cart, champagne cart, and cheese carts make a stately procession through the room; wine list exceeds 2,800 selections.
4. Gordon Ramsay trained under Guy Savoy and calls him his culinary mentor; Savoy voiced sous-chef Horst in the French version of Pixar's *Ratatouille*.
