## 2. TORALI — OPEN (The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago — NOT the Waldorf Astoria)

### ⚠️ Location correction
Torali is the signature restaurant on the **12th floor of The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago** (160 E Pearson St), not the Waldorf Astoria Chicago (11 E Walton St). Every source confirms this: toralichicago.com, ritzcarlton.com-hosted menus, OpenTable ("Torali - located at The Ritz Carlton, Chicago"), and FSR magazine ("signature restaurant on the 12th floor of The Ritz-Carlton, Chicago"). The Waldorf Astoria Chicago's on-site dining is The Brasserie / Peacock Lounge.

### Open verdict: OPEN
**Evidence:**
- OpenTable listing active and taking reservations (crawled 1–2 days before 2026-10-01); rating **4.4 (538 reviews)**; "Booked 8 times today".
- Verified 2026 diner reviews with management responses: Michele (dined July 11, 2026; responded July 17, 2026 by Leo Enriquez, Director of Food & Beverage), Himali (dined July 5, 2026), Carey (dined July 3, 2026; responded July 6, 2026).

- Current menus hosted on ritzcarlton.com (dinner menu PDF fetched 2026-10-01 directly).
