## 1. Chateau Marmont Restaurant

- **Address:** 8221 Sunset Boulevard, West Hollywood, CA 90046 (OpenTable listing; official site gives 8221 Sunset Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90046)
- **Phone:** +1 323 656 1010 (official site chateaumarmont.com)
- **Official URL:** https://www.chateaumarmont.com/
- **Cuisine:** American (OpenTable). Dining style "Casual Elegant" (OpenTable)
- **Price level:** OpenTable lists "$30 and under" (listing appears stale; Gayot rates it $$$$$)
- **Chef:** Executive Chef Dean Yasharian (OpenTable listing)
- **Hours (OpenTable):** Breakfast Mon–Fri 6:30–11:00 AM, Sat–Sun 6:30 AM–12:00 PM; Lunch Mon–Fri 11:00 AM–5:00 PM, Sat–Sun 12:00–5:00 PM; Dinner daily 5:00 PM–12:00 midnight
- **Reservations:** Official site links restaurant reservations to Resy ("Please call ahead for any restaurant or bar reservations. +1 323 656 1010"). Not bookable on OpenTable.
- **Dress code:** Smart Casual (OpenTable). Valet parking.
- **Reported dishes with prices** (source: OpenTable menu, **menu last updated 2/4/2022 — DATED**, prices likely changed; also menuwithprice.com breakfast menu, crawl 2026, undated):
  - Iceberg Wedge $17.00 — cherry tomatoes, bacon, walnuts, blue cheese dressing
  - Grilled Octopus $22.00 — watermelon, wild arugula, lemon vinaigrette
  - Bouillabaisse $35.00 — saffron fish broth, tiger prawns, mussels, rouille
  - Black Truffle Risotto $20.00 — carnaroli rice, sautéed mushrooms, shaved parmesan
  - Lamb $42.00 — couscous, piquillo, braised rapini, sunchokes, natural jus
  - Tapioca Pudding $12.00 — spiced pineapple, lime financier, coconut sorbet
  - Buttermilk Fried Chicken $29.00 (Sundays) — kale, mashed potatoes & gravy, biscuit, spiced honey
  - Charcuterie & Cheese $26.00 — cured meats, artisanal cheeses, pickles, celeriac rémoulade
  - Steak Frites $46.00 — dry-aged NY strip, french fries, béarnaise sauce
  - Chateau Burger $23.00 — cheddar, lettuce, tomato, house pickle, french fries
  - King Salmon $33.00 / White Sea Bass $35.00 / Roasted Chicken $32.00
  - Caesar Salad $16.00 / Beef Carpaccio $21.00 / Crispy Calamari $16.00 / Mushroom Ravioli $17.00 / Bucatini & Clams $16.00 / Spaghetti Bolognese $15.00 / Falafel $26.00
  - Breakfast (menuwithprice.com, undated): Eggs Benedict $22, Huevos Rancheros $22, Breakfast Burrito $22, Bagel & Lox $22, Croque Madame $20, Three Egg Omelette $21, Buttermilk Pancakes $17, Chateau Granola $15, coffee $6–10
- **Notable facts (for original intro copy):**
  - Built 1929 as an apartment building modeled on a Loire Valley royal residence; converted to a hotel two years later during the Depression (1010parkplace.com; seeing-stars.com)
  - Legendary celebrity haunt: guests have included Greta Garbo, Marilyn Monroe, Clark Gable, Elizabeth Taylor, Jim Morrison, Mick Jagger, Led Zeppelin; John Belushi died in Bungalow 3 in 1982 (1010parkplace.com; seeing-stars.com; travelweekly.com.au)
  - Columbia Pictures founder Harry Cohn's famous line: "If you must get in trouble, do it at the Chateau Marmont" (travelweekly.com.au)
- **UNVERIFIED:** Current (2026) menu prices — the only itemized menu found is dated 2/4/2022; current chef credit beyond OpenTable listing; exact current hours (OpenTable hours undated).

## 2. Polo Lounge (The Beverly Hills Hotel)

- **Address:** 9641 Sunset Boulevard, Beverly Hills, CA 90210 (official dorchestercollection.com page)
- **Phone:** +1 310 887 2777 (official page)
- **Official URL:** https://www.dorchestercollection.com/los-angeles/the-beverly-hills-hotel/dining/polo-lounge
- **Cuisine:** "American cuisine with an Italian edge" (official site). OpenTable: American, Californian; Fine Dining; price "$50 and over"
- **Price level:** $$$$ (official page)
- **Hours (official page):** Breakfast 7–11am; Lunch 11am–3pm; Brunch Sunday 11am–3pm; Dinner 5–11pm
- **Sunday Jazz Brunch:** $165 per person, three-course prix fixe incl. one complimentary beverage, Sundays 11am–3pm (official "Jazz Brunch" page, crawled 2026-10-01 — current)
- **Reservations:** Accepted online via official reservation page (location.com, crawled 2026-09-30); location.com also notes no reservations taken 3:00–4:30pm; last seating Sun–Thu 8:30pm, Fri–Sat 9:30pm (unverified on official site — flag if used)
- **Dress code (official page, current):** No baseball hats, beanies, ripped/shredded denim, crop tops, nightwear, swimwear, men's sleeveless shirts, offensive messages; sweatshirts/hoodies OK without hood up; after 4pm no shorts for men (ladies: tailored non-denim shorts OK); no flip-flops incl. Birkenstocks, no sportswear/tracksuits. Not applicable to children under 12.
- **Entertainment:** Piano accompaniment at lunch and dinner daily; jazz trio on the patio during Sunday brunch (OpenTable)
- **Reported dishes with prices:**
  - **McCarthy Salad — ~$40** (globetrender.com, 2020 article — DATED). Signature dish since the 1940s, named for polo player Neil McCarthy; ~90,000 sold per year per 2014 report (missmanifa.com — DATED); ~600 orders/week per Modern Luxury (Dec 2024). A $1,912 anniversary edition (gold leaf, lobster, caviar, Dom Pérignon, take-home Bernardaud bowl) was offered Dec 2024 for the hotel's 110th anniversary (modernluxury.com, Dec 2024).
  - Polo Lounge Avocado Toast $35.80 / Southern California Omelet $34.80 / Green Market Scrambled Eggs $35.80 / Hollywood Farmers' Market Seasonal Fruit $29.80 / Classic Cereal with Bananas $19.90 (menulist.menu, **menu data ~Jan 2025 — DATED**)
  - Other reported signature items (prices UNVERIFIED): Chocolate Soufflé (served on a "red carpet" per reviews), Dover Sole Meuniere, "Polo"-branded American Wagyu Burger, Chicken Tortilla Soup, Steak Tartare, Lemon Blueberry Ricotta Pancakes, Ahi Tuna Tartare, Old Fashioned Chocolate Cake (hautelivingsf.com May 2025; sluurpy.us reviews; dorchestercollection place data)
- **Notable facts:**
  - The Beverly Hills Hotel opened 1912 ("Pink Palace"); Polo Lounge has been "the favorite breakfast spot and watering hole for generations of stars and Hollywood deal-makers" (official site)
  - The McCarthy salad has been on the menu untouched for decades; photography is frowned upon inside (celebrity discretion culture) (hautelivingsf.com)
  - OpenTable rating 4.6 (1,377 reviews); official rating 4.4 (771 reviews)
- **UNVERIFIED:** Current per-dish prices for lunch/dinner menu (official site publishes no prices); afternoon tea price/schedule (one Q&A cited $75 Fri–Sat 3–4pm but source is old — do not publish without fresh verification).

## 3. CUT by Wolfgang Puck (Beverly Wilshire)

- **Address:** 9500 Wilshire Blvd, Beverly Hills, CA 90212 (official wolfgangpuck.com)
- **Phone:** (310) 276-8500 (official page); private dining (310) 777-3711
- **Official URL:** https://wolfgangpuck.com/restaurants/cut-beverly-hills/
- **Cuisine:** Modern American steakhouse (official: "contemporary twist on the classic steakhouse")
- **Price level:** Very expensive / "$50 and over" (OpenTable)
- **Chef:** Executive Chef Drew Rosenberg; GM Ian Curtis (official page)
- **Hours (official page):** Closed Sun–Mon; Tue–Thu 5:30–10:00 PM; Fri–Sat 5:00–10:30 PM (CUT Lounge to 11:00 PM Fri–Sat)
- **Reservations:** "Reservations are recommended"; bookable via OpenTable (official page links OpenTable; OpenTable shows it "Booked 25 times today" Sep 2026)
- **Dress code:** Business Casual (OpenTable). Valet $15/3hrs validated (OpenTable).
- **Reported dishes with prices:**
  - **"The First CUT" early-evening prix fixe: $95/person** (3 courses + side; Tue–Thu 5:30–6:30pm; Apr 7–Dec 31, 2026; optional $85 wine pairing) — OpenTable + Haute Living, June 2026 (CURRENT)
  - 20th Anniversary "World of CUT" 7-course dinner: $295/person (one night, Aug 20, 2026 — past; useful as price context only)
  - Signature dishes widely reported but **current per-dish prices UNVERIFIED** (only priced menus found are kevinEats blog posts from 2008–2012 — DATED, e.g. Bone Marrow Flan $17 in 2008, Japanese Wagyu NY $135 in 2012): Bone Marrow Flan (mushroom marmalade, parsley salad), Lobster & Crab "Louis" Cocktail, Snake River Farms Wagyu Tomahawk, Butter Lettuce Salad, Crabcake, Brandt NY Strip, Prime Sirloin Steak Tartare, Creamed Spinach with Egg, French Fries, Baked Alaska, Basque Cheesecake, Banana Cream Pie, Dark Chocolate Soufflé (official descriptions + 2026 OpenTable reviews)
- **Notable facts:**
  - The original/flagship CUT (opened 2006; celebrated 20th anniversary June 2026); Michelin star 2019 and 2021 (official page); OpenTable Icon 2026
  - Designed by architect Richard Meier (Getty Center); art by John Baldessari and Martin Schoeller (OpenTable)
  - OpenTable rating 4.7 (2,974 reviews)
- **UNVERIFIED:** Current à la carte per-dish prices (not published on official site or OpenTable).

## 4. Sierra Mar (Post Ranch Inn)

- **Address:** 47900 State Highway 1, Big Sur, CA 93920 (OpenTable)
- **Phone:** (831) 667-2800; toll-free 1-800-527-2200 (hautelivingsf.com)
- **Official URL:** https://www.postranchinn.com/dining (official dining page)
- **Cuisine:** Organic, Californian, farm-to-table (OpenTable)
- **Price level:** $50 and over / $$$$
- **Chef:** Executive Chef Il Hoon Kang (OpenTable, current; previously at Michelin-starred Madera, Commis, The Restaurant at Meadowood — hautelivingsf.com, May 2023)
- **Hours (OpenTable, crawled 2026-09-30 — current):** Lunch daily 12:00–3:00 PM; Dinner daily 5:30–9:00 PM
- **Pricing (OpenTable, current):** Family-style lunch experience $85/person; dinner experience $185/person (seasonal tasting menu); supplemental sustainable caviar service from $220 (NY Post/US Times Mirror, July 2026)
- **Reservations:** Advance reservations required; bookable on OpenTable ("Booked 29 times today" Sep 2026); non-hotel guests admitted at gatehouse max 30 min before reservation
- **Dress code:** Smart Casual (OpenTable). Valet + private lot.
- **Dishes:** Prix-fixe only — no à la carte dish prices published. Reported menu style: seasonal tasting menus "centered around the dialogue of balance: on the plate, between sea and land, with sustainability always in mind"; partnerships with Ad Astra Bread Co., The Cheese Shop (Carmel), Brokaw Farms (Soledad), Monterey Abalone (Monterey County Weekly, Best Restaurant Big Sur 2025). Wine Spectator Grand Award list — 3,200+ selections, 12,000-bottle cellar (hautelivingsf.com, 2023).
- **Notable facts:**
  - Michelin Green Star for environmental excellence (NY Post, July 2026; note: NOT a Michelin culinary star — the articles explicitly say it has "not been given a culinary constellation")
  - Wine Spectator Grand Award — the only Grand Award in Monterey County (hautelivingsf.com)
  - Named Best Restaurant in Big Sur 2025 (Monterey County Weekly readers' poll)
  - Post Ranch Inn opened 1992 (fyple listing); cliffs above the Pacific on Highway 1
- **UNVERIFIED:** Individual dish names/prices (tasting menu changes regularly; nothing current published).

## 5. Addison by William Bradley (Fairmont Grand Del Mar)

- **Address:** 5200 Grand Del Mar Way, San Diego, CA 92130 (official addisondelmar.com)
- **Phone:** 858-314-1900 (official site)
- **Official URL:** http://www.addisondelmar.com/ (also on fairmont.com)
- **Cuisine:** "California Gastronomy" (official site)
- **Price level:** Very expensive / $$$$
- **Chef:** Chef William Bradley (Relais & Châteaux Grand Chef)
- **Hours:** Tue–Sat 5:00–9:00 PM (place data); diningwithfrankie.com (Aug 2026) reports Tue–Sat with last seating 8:30 PM. **Flag:** hours not confirmed on official site — use with caveat.
- **Pricing (official site, current):**
  - Ten-course tasting menu: **$398/person** (addisondelmar.com "The Experience" page + fairmont.com)
  - Champagne Lounge: Tartelette Menu $198/guest, optional Champagne pairings $175; walk-in only, first-come first-served
  - Older report (diningwithfrankie.com, Aug 2026): $385/person — use $398 as the current official figure
- **Reservations:** Via official website reservation form (addisondelmar.com); advance booking strongly advised ("book months ahead" per reviews)
- **Dress code:** UNVERIFIED on official site (FAQ page is JS-driven, answer not retrievable). Dated third-party listing (partycache.com) says "Jacket Preferred" — flag as dated if used.
- **Reported signature dishes** (no per-dish prices — tasting menu only; from wanderlog, diningwithfrankie Aug 2026, fairmont.com):
  - Chicken liver churro with bitter chocolate (appetizer)
  - "Eggs and rice" — Regiis Ova Reserve caviar, Koshihikari rice, smoked sabayon, sesame
  - Tom kha goong with king crab (sweet-and-sour take)
  - Kinmedai with crab consommé
  - Uni chawanmushi
  - Barbecued squab
  - Iberian ham with crispy potato
  - Optional supplements reported: caviar course add-on, wagyu upgrade on quail course (prices not published — UNVERIFIED)
- **Notable facts:**
  - Southern California's first and only three-Michelin-star restaurant (Michelin 3 stars 2024, 2025, 2026 per OpenTable; official site)
  - Celebrating 20th anniversary with newly redesigned dining room + new Champagne Lounge (fairmont.com)
  - Relais & Châteaux Grand Chef William Bradley; Wine Spectator Grand Award wine program (~10,000-bottle cellar per place data)
  - OpenTable rating 4.8-class; pricey wine pairings noted by reviewers
- **UNVERIFIED:** Exact current operating hours; dress code; supplement/add-on prices.

## 6. Tonga Room & Hurricane Bar (Fairmont San Francisco)

- **Address:** 950 Mason Street, San Francisco, CA 94108 (official tongaroom.com)
- **Phone:** +1 415 772 5278 (official site; hotel main +1 415 772 5000)
- **Official URL:** http://www.tongaroom.com/
- **Cuisine:** Asian-fusion / Polynesian-fusion; classic tiki bar (official site)
- **Price level:** $ / "$$" per official site (modest); OpenTable lists "$30 and under"; restaurantguru reports $50–100/person range — treat as $$.
- **Hours (official site, current):** Wed–Thu 5:00–10:00 PM; Fri–Sat 5:00–11:00 PM. Live music from 7:00 PM. All ages 5–10 PM; 21+ after 10 PM.
- **Reservations:** Limited dinner reservations via Tablz app (official site); first-come first-served seating also available. (Note: an older venue description says reservations not accepted except hotel guests — the current official site shows Tablz booking, so use the current info.)
- **Cover charge:** $20/person once live music starts (applies to all seated guests regardless of arrival time; waived for registered Fairmont hotel guests) — official site, current. Older sources cite $15 — use $20.
- **Dress code:** Casual Dress (OpenTable)
- **Reported dishes with prices** (source: official Fairmont SF menu PDF "SAF-Tonga-Menu-3_14", **dated ~Mar 2024** — prices may have changed):
  - Pork Siu Mai $15 — steamed pork and shrimp dumplings
  - Vegetable Pot Stickers $14 — cabbage, black mushroom, onion, ginger
  - Coconut Shell Spicy Tuna Poke $18 — ahi tuna, macadamia nuts, scallion, taro chips
  - Peel & Eat Prawns $12 — wok-fried, jalapeño, garlic, ginger, yuzu mayo
  - Curried Crab Won Tacos $16 — dungeness crab, yellow curry, pickled daikon
  - Mūhe'e (crispy calamari) $15 — scallions, herbs, ponzu aioli
  - Fresh Rice Paper Spring Rolls $16
  - Ahi Tuna Poke Tostada $28 / Tuna Poke Salad $28
  - Tonga Grilled Ribeye $54
  - Marinated Korean Short Rib $26 / Citrus Pepper Chicken Wings $26 / Fried Vegetable Spring Rolls $26
  - Tonga Pupu Platter $66
  - Tonga Caesar Salad $22 / Asian Mixed Greens $18 / Soy Lime Edamame $20
  - Signature cocktails (names verified; **prices UNVERIFIED**): Mai Tai 1944, Zombie (five rums), Tiki Hunter, Wiki Wiki, Hand Shaken Colada, Tiny Bubbles. A Jan 2025 cocktail review noted $2–4 per-cocktail price increases (ultimatemaitai.com — DATED).
- **Notable facts:**
  - Opened 1945: the hotel's former 75-foot indoor swimming pool ("The Plunge," built 1929) was converted by MGM set designer Mel Melvin into a tropical escape; indoor rainstorms (thunder, lightning, rain over the lagoon) added in the 1950s, still running every ~30 minutes (secretsanfrancisco.com; official site)
  - Anthony Bourdain called it "the greatest place in the history of the world" (official site; multiple sources)
  - Band "The Island Groove" plays from a floating boat stage; named "most unusual restaurant in California" by LoveFood (iheart.com, Oct 2024); "San Francisco's Best Happy Hour" awards (venue's own description — flag as self-claimed)
- **UNVERIFIED:** Current cocktail prices; whether the March 2024 menu prices are still current (official site says "menu is subject to change").
