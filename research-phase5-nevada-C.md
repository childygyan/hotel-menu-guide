## Notable, verifiable facts (for original intro copy)
1. **World's first Nobu Hotel:** Nobu Hotel at Caesars Palace opened in 2013 as the first-ever Nobu Hotel worldwide — a venture between chef Nobu Matsuhisa, Caesars Palace, Robert De Niro, and film producer Meir Teper. The hotel itself (designed by David Rockwell) was named "Top North America Hotel Opening in 2013" by Luxury Travel Advisor.
2. **Largest Nobu restaurant space:** At opening it was reported as the world's largest Nobu restaurant (12,775 sq ft at opening per 2014 coverage; current sources describe the dining area + lounge as 11,200 sq ft) — inside the Nobu Hotel on the Caesars Palace grounds.
3. **Only U.S. Nobu with teppan tables:** This is the only Nobu location in the United States offering interactive teppanaki/teppan tables, where the Teppanyaki Omakase is prepared tableside.
4. **Successor to a Michelin-starred location:** The Caesars Palace Nobu replaced Nobu's previous Las Vegas home at the old Hard Rock (now Virgin Hotels) — the one that held a Michelin star in 2008–2009 — and brought the brand to the Strip's most famous resort.
5. OpenTable has awarded it "OpenTable Icons" status in 2024, 2025, and 2026.
