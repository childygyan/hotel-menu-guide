3. **The "Not So Secret" Secret Burger** is the signature dish (pimento cheese, gouda, bacon jam, B&B pickles, salsa macha aioli — official bar-snacks menu, prices not published).
4. **Hotel context:** housed in the historic Chicago Athletic Association (1893 club building, Henry Ives Cobb architect), which Boka Restaurant Group took over food & beverage operations for in March 2025 (NRN, May 2026).
5. **Dessert note:** the official 2022 menu featured a S'mores Baked Alaska ($10 in spring 2022 PDF) — useful dated price anchor only.

## 3. SUBSTITUTE: THE IVES — OPEN ✅ (same hotel, same space)

### Open evidence (2026)
- Opened May 19, 2026 per NRN (article published ~May 2026, crawled 1 hour before research).
- Active OpenTable listing: rating **4.5 (56 reviews)**; "Booked 24 times today" per Sept 29, 2026 availability; reviews dined **Aug 29, 2026** (OpenTable search results, crawled ~1 day before research).
- Active official page on bokagrp.com (crawled 4 days before research): "The Ives is a modern grill room inspired by the architectural and cultural legacy of the historic Chicago Athletic Association."

### Hotel / address / phone / cuisine / chef / hours
- **Hotel:** Chicago Athletic Association, **12 S Michigan Ave, Chicago, IL 60603** (OpenTable listing, 2026). Cross street Madison & Michigan; valet parking; Downtown/Loop.
- **Phone:** not shown on verified sources seen — DO NOT publish a phone number.
- **Cuisine:** American, Prime Rib, Dining Bar (OpenTable 2026); described as a **modern grill room / modern version of the classic supper club** (NRN, May 2026; Chicago Magazine, May 2026).
- **Chef:** **Chef-partner Chris Pandel** (also behind BRG's Swift & Sons, Cira, Zarella Pizzeria & Taverna — NRN, May 2026; listed as Executive Chef on OpenTable 2026).
- **Hours (OpenTable 2026 listing):** Dinner only — Mon–Thu 5:00 pm–10:00 pm; Fri–Sat 5:00 pm–11:00 pm. (Closed Sun per listing.) Chicago Magazine (May 2026): "only open for dinner."
- **Price band:** $50 and over (OpenTable 2026). Dress: Smart Casual. Events: TheIvesEvents@caa.bokagrp.com.

### Signature dishes + price bands (all dated)
- **Wagyu prime rib, carved tableside — signature entrée: menu said $82, bill was $84** (OpenTable review, dined Aug 29, 2026; reviewer "Crista"). Prime rib is A4 wagyu, cured for days, slow-cooked with peppercorn crust, jus seasoned with star anise, cinnamon, and chiles (Chicago Magazine, May 2026).
- **Relish cart: $38 per person** (roving cart of rotating small bites — mini sandwiches, deviled eggs, spring onion tea sandwiches, chicken liver mousse — NRN, May 2026).
- **Sides:** potato $14; wilted greens $12 (same Aug 2026 OpenTable review; sides are extra, "forcing patrons to buy side dishes").
- **Surcharge note:** 4% surcharge on the bill (same Aug 2026 review) — label as one reviewer's report, not official policy.
- Other verified dishes (no prices): beef tartare with celery and mustard; baked Jonah crab dip with artichoke and potato chips; "1909 salad" with frisée, cress, and deviled ham; burger; French dip; roast half chicken; pork chop schnitzel with dill, caper, and horseradish; strip steak with black truffle jus; filet mignon au poivre (OpenTable blog / Chicago Magazine / NRN, May–Aug 2026).
- Desserts (no prices): baked Alaska flamed tableside; chocolate toast (crispy bread, chocolate shaved tableside); chocolate trolley finished by executive pastry chef Kristine Antonian; key lime tart; banana crème brûlée (Chicago Magazine May 2026; OpenTable blog; Aug 2026 review).
- Bar snacks: crispy smelts with tartar sauce, smoked whitefish dip, housemade ranch crudités, housemade chicken nuggets (Chicago Magazine, May 2026).
- Cocktails: Old Fashioned, Hemingway Daiquiri, New York Sour, Dirty Martini; beverage manager Javaughn Marshall (NRN, May 2026).

### Awards / credentials
- None yet — opened May 2026, too new. Operated by **Boka Restaurant Group** (James Beard Award-winning group per Culinary Agents, 2026); chef-partner Chris Pandel runs BRG's Swift & Sons, Cira, and Zarella Pizzeria & Taverna.
- Name origin: named for **Henry Ives Cobb**, the Chicago Athletic Association building's original architect (official bokagrp.com page + OpenTable, 2026).

### Notable facts for an original writeup
1. **Supper-club revival concept:** "a modern version of the classic supper club" — tableside carved wagyu prime rib, roving relish cart ($38/person), Champagne cart, chocolate trolley, tableside-flamed baked Alaska (NRN + Chicago Magazine, May 2026).
2. **Same bones, new green:** Boka kept the room's iconic giant wrap-around bar and architectural bones (more green accents, less carpet) — continuity with the Cherry Circle Room era (Chicago Magazine, May 2026).
3. **Named for the architect:** "The Ives" honors Henry Ives Cobb, original architect of the 1893 Chicago Athletic Association building (official page, 2026).
4. **BRG's second act at the hotel:** after taking over F&B in March 2025, Boka first opened Midōsuji (eight-seat omakase, Sept 2025), then converted the Cherry Circle Room into The Ives (NRN, May 2026).
