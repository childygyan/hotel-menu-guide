### 1e. Notable facts for the writeup
1. **1930s Shanghai supper-club setting** — the dining rooms are done in ebony, gold, and scarlet with mahogany quatrefoil screens, evoking Old Shanghai glamour; multiple sources describe it as one of the most atmospheric Chinese rooms in Chicago.
2. **The outdoor terrace** — in warmer months seating moves to an urban-garden terrace with skyline views (per OpenTable: "additional seating is provided on The Terrace, an urban garden with spectacular skyline views"); in cooler months the hotel's soaring Lobby hosts overflow diners. Reservations are recommended and guests are asked to state seating preference.
3. **Private dining room** for up to 30 guests with set menus (OpenTable).
4. **Mooncake tradition** — the Peninsula's signature handmade egg-custard mooncakes are made in-house, a legacy of Dim Sum Ambassador Chef Yip's 26-year tenure at The Peninsula Hong Kong's Spring Moon (reported in a 2015 food-blogger visit; the mid-autumn mooncake demonstration was led by Chef Elmo Han).

### 2e. Notable facts for the writeup
1. **Mies van der Rohe architecture + river views** — the restaurant sits on the 2nd floor of the landmark IBM Building (330 N Wabash), with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking the Chicago River; Chicago Magazine's opening review noted the glassed-in open kitchen and the striking lounge with its 30-foot-long video screen designed as a "beacon" for drivers on the Wabash Avenue Bridge.
2. **All-day American brasserie model** — one of few downtown fine-casual rooms serving breakfast through late-night lounge dinner seven days a week, with a dedicated weekend brunch (lobster benedict, oyster program, caviar service) and a kids menu.
3. **2026 activity confirms ongoing relevance** — March 2026 St Patrick's Day brunch packages and the summer 2026 "Ice Cream on the River" riverside pop-up show the property actively programming food events.
4. **Chef lineage** — opened 2013 under Tim Graham (Tru); later chefs include Jeff Vucko and Justin Gomes; current executive chef per the live OpenTable listing is Franco Diaz.
