# Phase 5 (Nevada B) Research — Las Vegas Hotel Restaurants

Research date: 2026-10-01. Methods: web search + text fetches of search-returned pages.
No live-browser visits were performed. Prices are reported facts with their date/era;
nothing is invented. Note: `guide.michelin.com` blocks automated text fetches in this
environment, so Michelin status is verified from web search coverage of the
inaugural 2026 MICHELIN Guide Southwest (announced late August 2026), not from the
Guide's own pages.

## 1. Le Cirque — Bellagio — ⚠️ PERMANENTLY CLOSED

**Open-status verification:** Le Cirque is NOT open. It permanently closed after its
final dinner service on **Sunday, August 23, 2026**, announced by MGM Resorts/Bellagio
in April 2026 (sources: FOX5/KVVU, Las Vegas Review-Journal 2026-04-13, TheStreet,
Parade, eatscv.com). The space will become a new culinary concept expected mid-2027;
as of Sept 2026 MGM had not publicly announced the replacement (vegas411.com).
This was the brand's final U.S. location (NYC flagship shuttered Dec 2017).
Wikipedia's list of Michelin-starred Las Vegas restaurants also marks it "Closed (2026)".

- **Official restaurant page (was):** https://bellagio.mgmresorts.com/en/restaurants/le-cirque.html
  (also verbatim from search results: https://bellagio.mgmresorts.com/en/restaurants/le-cirque.html?ecid=GMB)
- **Hotel page:** Bellagio Resort & Casino — bellagio.mgmresorts.com (restaurant page sits under this domain)
- **Reservations:** none available — the restaurant has closed; it was formerly bookable via Bellagio's dining page.
- **Michelin:** no current Michelin listing (guide returned to Vegas only in 2026 and
  Le Cirque closed the same month). Historic: one Michelin star in the 2008–2009
  Las Vegas guide era; also Forbes Five-Star, AAA Five Diamond, Wine Spectator Best
  of Award of Excellence (FOX5/KVVU, April 2026).
- **Address:** 3600 Las Vegas Blvd S, Las Vegas, NV 89109
- **Phone:** (702) 693-8100
- **Executive chef:** Dameon Evers (chef since the October 28, 2021 post-Covid reopening;
  previously with Thomas Keller's Bouchon and Gordon Ramsay West Hollywood)
- **Hours (final era, per OpenTable listing):** Mon, Thu–Sun 5:00 pm–10:00 pm (Tue–Wed closed)
- **Cuisine:** French / contemporary French fine dining. Price: $$$$ / "very expensive"
- **Signature/known dishes with prices (dated):**
  - Farewell prix fixe menus, summer 2026 (per vegas411.com, updated ~Sept 2026):
    3-course $185 / 4-course $255 / 6-course dégustation $310 per person;
    optional wine pairings $150–$290, Champagne Flight $270 (before tax & gratuity).
    Dishes named: lobster salad, "Black Tie" (truffle jus, Hokkaido scallop or baby
    spinach), Brittany Loup De Mer, Grilled Wagyu Filet, Chocolate Ball dessert.
  - 2021 reopening menus (per snaptaste.com, Oct 2021): ten-course $388;
    six-course vegetarian $288; pre-theater 3-course $128 (5–6 pm).
  - April 2026 reporting (TheStreet): dining "experience" $200–$400 per person before
    wine; four-course tasting menus in the $700–$800 range; special menus $1,900+ per person.
  - A la carte prices in the final era were not published in any source found.
- **Notable, verifiable facts for intro copy:**
  1. Opened with Bellagio in October 1998; after nearly 28 years on the Strip it closed
     Aug 23, 2026 — the last U.S. Le Cirque.
  2. Founded by hospitality legend Sirio Maccioni (the NYC original dates to 1974;
     Maccioni died 2020).
  3. Dining room designed by Adam D. Tihany with a colorful circus-tent-like,
     jewel-toned interior; fountain-side tables overlook the Fountains of Bellagio.
  4. Final menus featured modern riffs on French classics under Executive Chef Dameon
     Evers, including the lobster salad, "Black Tie" truffle preparation, and the
     iconic Chocolate Ball dessert.

## 2. Carbone — ARIA Resort & Casino — OPEN

- **Official restaurant page:** https://aria.mgmresorts.com/en/restaurants/carbone.html
- **Hotel page:** ARIA Resort & Casino — www.aria.com (restaurant page also served from aria.com;
  verbatim official page found under the aria.mgmresorts.com host)
- **Reservations:** NOT on the OpenTable booking network — OpenTable's own listing states
  "this restaurant is not on the OpenTable booking network. To check availability,
  please contact them directly." Book via the ARIA site's reservation flow
  (https://aria.mgmresorts.com/en/restaurants/carbone.html) or by phone.
  OpenTable listing (reviews only): https://www.opentable.com/carbone-las-vegas
- **Michelin:** no Michelin Guide listing and no star for the Las Vegas outpost.
  (The NYC original received a Michelin star in the 2014 NYC guide; the 2026
  inaugural MICHELIN Guide Southwest starred four Vegas restaurants — Joël Robuchon
  (2★), é by José Andrés, L'Atelier de Joël Robuchon, Guy Savoy (1★ each) — none is Carbone.)
- **Address:** 3730 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89158
- **Phone:** 702.590.2663 (aria.mgmresorts.com)
- **Executive chef:** Jonah Resnick (per the official MultiVu press fact sheet;
  OpenTable also lists Jonah Resnick)
- **Hours:** Sun–Thu 5:00 pm–10:30 pm; Fri–Sat 5:00 pm–11:00 pm (per aria.mgmresorts.com)
- **Cuisine:** Italian-American fine dining ("traditional Italian-American cuisine
  elevated into the fine dining realm"). Price: $$$$ ("very expensive"; ~$100+ per person)
- **Signature/known dishes with prices (dated):**
  - Per the official MultiVu press fact sheet (era uncertain — appears to be an older
    press kit; treat as dated/not current): appetizers $18–$45; main courses $37–$120;
    signature cocktails $15; wine by the glass $15–$35.
  - Mid-2025 reporting (vegasenews.com): Spicy Rigatoni Vodka ~$36; Veal Parmesan ~$65;
    antipasti $20–$40; pasta $30–$55; main courses $50–$75; sides $15–$25; desserts $15–$25.
  - Signature dishes (no price published): Chicken Scarpariello, Lobster Fra Diavola
    (Diavolo), Octopus Pizzaiolo, Veal Parmesan, tableside Caesar salad, bananas foster
    flambe, tableside rum cart of rare vintage rums.
- **Notable, verifiable facts for intro copy:**
  1. Opened October 2015 at ARIA's Promenade level; the Vegas outpost of the 2013
     Greenwich Village original by Major Food Group (Mario Carbone, Rich Torrisi,
     Jeff Zalaznick). A second Vegas sibling, Carbone Riviera, opened at Bellagio in late 2025.
  2. Designed by Ken Fulk as a mid-century Italian-American supper club — red leather
     booths, tuxedoed captains, tableside service and showmanship.
  3. 550-label Italian wine list with vintages back to 1929, plus a signature tableside
     vintage-rum cart (Black Tot British Royal Naval Rum, Appleton 50-Year Rum).
  4. Consistently among the Strip's hardest reservations — book 30 days out; OpenTable
     rating 4.7 from ~1,900 reviews.

## 3. Jean Georges Steakhouse — ARIA Resort & Casino — OPEN

- **Official restaurant page:** https://www.aria.com/en/restaurants/jean-georges-steakhouse.html
- **Hotel page:** ARIA Resort & Casino — www.aria.com
- **Reservations:** Bookable on OpenTable:
  https://www.opentable.com/r/jean-georges-steakhouse-aria-mgm-resorts
  (also https://www.opentable.com/restaurant/profile/90895/reserve?rid=90895&ref=9601 —
  OpenTable shows live bookable slots, 4.7 from ~2,100 reviews)
- **Michelin:** no Michelin Guide listing found for the ARIA steakhouse.
  IMPORTANT DISTINCTION: the Michelin-starred "Jean-Georges" at Waldorf Astoria
  Las Vegas (23rd floor) is a different restaurant — the ARIA steakhouse earned no
  star in the 2026 inaugural Southwest guide.
- **Address:** 3730 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89158
- **Phone:** (702) 590-8660
- **Executive chef:** Jean-Georges Vongerichten (chef/owner; "Meet the chef" on the
  official ARIA page)
- **Hours (per aria.mgmresorts.com):** Mon–Tue 5:00 pm–10:30 pm; Wed–Thu CLOSED;
  Fri–Sun 5:00 pm–10:30 pm. Dress code: smart casual (no athletic/beach wear).
- **Cuisine:** Steakhouse / American / seafood. Price: $$$$ ("very expensive")
- **Signature/known dishes with prices (dated):**
  - Circa spring 2023 (menucollectors.com; page marked "updated" ~1,251 days before
    Oct 2026 ≈ April 2023): F1 Wagyu Filet 6 oz $130; F1 Wagyu NY Strip 10 oz $155;
    F1 Wagyu Ribeye 16 oz $190; 36 oz Prime Porterhouse $141; JG Cheeseburger $36;
    Chilean Sea Bass $51; Seared Salmon $49; sides (black truffle mac n cheese, grilled
    asparagus, trumpet mushrooms, etc.) $18 each; Oysters on the Half Shell $28;
    Crispy Sushi Sampler $25; Seared Foie Gras $32; Bacon Wrapped Shrimp $30.
    (Note: separate restaurantguru.com menu listing shows similar but undated prices.)
  - 2011-era blog report (greateatshawaii.com): Chef's Tasting Menu $85 — historical only.
  - Current published menu prices could not be verified — treat the 2023 figures as the
    latest dated reference, not as current.
- **Notable, verifiable facts for intro copy:**
  1. Jean-Georges Vongerichten's modern steakhouse at ARIA — steaks grilled over
     sustainable apricot wood and mesquite, finished with rendered beef fat.
  2. Japanese certified beef program: F1 wagyu cuts plus Hyogo Prefecture A5 Kobe —
     one of very few U.S. restaurants licensed to serve A5 Kobe; tableside-carved
     shareable cuts including the 42 oz Wagyu Tomahawk and 32 oz porterhouse.
  3. Black-and-gold Art-Deco-inspired dining room entered through an Art-Deco gate;
     266 seats with private dining (Boardroom, Chef's Table, wine alcove), guests 7+ only.
  4. Menu blends steakhouse classics with JG's global flavors — crispy sushi sampler,
     Chilean sea bass, and the warm Valrhona chocolate cake are the frequently cited
     non-steak standouts.
