## 1. Terra — Four Seasons Resort Rancho Encantado Santa Fe
- **Restaurant:** Terra
- **Hotel:** Four Seasons Resort Rancho Encantado Santa Fe
- **Address:** 198 State Road 592, Santa Fe, NM 87506 (per OpenTable; resort also lists "198 State Road 592, Santa Fe, New Mexico 87506 U.S.A." on fourseasons.com/santafe)
- **Phone:** (505) 946-5800 (Terra, per OpenTable Sept 2026); resort main 1 (505) 946-5700
- **Executive chef:** Andrew Fox (per OpenTable, Sept 2026)

**Cuisine (own words):** Contemporary American with a northern New Mexican heart — red and green chile, bison, blue corn, piñon, and local produce — served in an elegant, art-filled dining room with Sangre de Cristo mountain views and a heated patio. **Price level: $$$$** (OpenTable typical band "$31 to $50" per person, Sept 2026).

**Hours — conflict noted:**

**Signature dishes (own words; no verbatim menu copying):**
1. Steak For Two (official "Signature Dish," Four Seasons page): tomahawk-style steak for two with grilled asparagus and green-chile mac and cheese sides.
2. Pepita Crumble Seared Scallops — a frequent guest favorite (OpenTable FAQ, Sept 2026).
3. Lamb shank — praised in Aug 2026 verified reviews ("crazy good").
4. Poblano leek soup — praised in Aug 2026 verified reviews.
5. Green-chile bison meatballs (TastingTable, 2023 write-up).
6. Rotating seasonal specials — fall 2026 "Seven Nights of Terra" rotation included Bison Tomahawk, Birria Tacos, Adovada Prime Rib, and fried chicken with green-chile mashed potatoes (TravelPRNews press, 2026-09-24).
7. Breakfast standouts (official breakfast menu PDF, crawled ~Mar 2026): Beef Sunrise Burrito, Santa Fe Style Chilaquiles, Encantado Sunrise Hash, Prickly Pear & Granola Parfait, Avocado Toast with crab meat.

**Published prices (each labeled):**

## 2. Julia — A Spirited Restaurant & Bar — La Posada de Santa Fe, a Tribute Portfolio Resort & Spa
- **Restaurant:** Julia — A Spirited Restaurant & Bar (named for Julia Staab, matriarch of the 1882 Staab mansion on the property)
- **Hotel:** La Posada de Santa Fe, A Tribute Portfolio Resort & Spa
- **Address:** 330 East Palace Avenue, Santa Fe, NM 87501 (per official Julia page + OpenTable)
- **Phone:** (505) 986-0000
- **Executive chef:** Chef Edgar Jiminez (per OpenTable, Sept 2026)

**Cuisine (own words):** Southwestern-American comfort dining inside a 19th-century Victorian mansion — polished local dishes with a full bar, crafted margaritas, and a garden patio with kiva fireplaces. **Price level: $$$$** (OpenTable typical band "$31 to $50" per person, Sept 2026; note: Julia's own site lists "$$" price range — conflicting, older source).

**Hours (official hotel Julia page, last updated ~Apr/May 2026; matches OpenTable Sept 2026):** Breakfast daily 7:00am–11:00am; Dinner daily 5:00pm–9:00pm. OpenTable also notes live Spanish-guitar performances in the bar on the first Wednesday of each month.

**Signature dishes (own words; date-labeled since current menu not publicly posted):**
1. Chicken corn chowder — recurring guest favorite, "appears on the current menu" (OpenTable FAQ, Sept 2026).
2. Roast Colorado lamb with stone-ground mustard and local honey (travel review era ~2016 — dated).
3. Dark chocolate ganache pueblo dessert (travel review era ~2016 — dated).
4. Crab enchiladas; Christmas tamales (La Posada blog "Upscale Dining," published ~Jan 2019 — dated).
5. Duck breast special — "as good as anything I have eaten in France" (OpenTable verified review, Aug 2026).
6. Seared scallops with bread-and-butters; beet salad; caramel cheesecake (OpenTable verified reviews, Aug 2026).
7. The Juliarita margarita — homemade apricot liqueur infused tequila, made from apricots still growing on a tree Julia herself planted in the 1880s (La Posada official dining page; served at the Staab House Bar and the Patio).

**Published prices (each labeled):**
- Vara Wine Dinner experience: $150 per person including tax and gratuity (OpenTable "Experiences," event date 2026-09-24 — one-time event).
- No published menu prices found; OpenTable typical spend "$31 to $50" per person (Sept 2026).

**Recognition/awards (attributed):**
- **Travel + Leisure Magazine named the Staab House "the coziest bar in Santa Fe"** — quoted on La Posada's official dining page (current as of Sept 2026 crawl). Note: this award belongs to the Staab House bar, a sibling venue, not Julia proper — do not attribute to Julia.
- James Beard award-winner Todd Hall was executive chef of Julia (hotel press page, ~2015 — historical, attribution: La Posada press archive).
- OpenTable rating 4.5 (498 reviews, Sept 2026); "Loved For": Romantic, Good for special occasions.

**Reservations:** OpenTable (verified live) + phone (505) 986-0000.
